# PCB evidence — missing primary board material

CG161P REV 2.00 and TU106-100-A1 are supplied identifiers, not independently verified photographs in this phase. No trace-level boardview, schematic, package pinout or authenticated photographs have been examined. No conclusion that signals reach package pins or connector pads is justified.

Required evidence: original high-resolution photographs of both PCB faces with legible board revision and chip marking; locate output pads, differential pairs, AUX/DDC, HPD, ESD components and any level shifters. Surface photographs alone cannot establish absent internal-layer routes. A schematic/boardview or appropriately documented continuity measurements would be stronger. Do not attach a connector or energize unknown pads as a diagnostic.

Empty DCB/connector tables are firmware board descriptions; they cannot establish BOARD_UNROUTED. A public photograph of another CMP board or a GeForce TU106 does not establish this board's routing.
